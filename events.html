Event Command Center

Click any event on the calendar to open it, review the pre-written reminder emails, and send. Attendees get reminders 3 & 2 days before; the PL gets her task checklist 1 week before.
🔒
Nothing sends without your approval. Every email starts as Needs approval — you open it, review and edit the wording, then choose to Approve. Only after that does a manual, passcode-protected Send now button appear. There is no automatic or background sending. Setting the Vercel variables (RESEND_API_KEY, RESEND_FROM, SEND_PASSCODE) only turns the Send button on — it never sends anything by itself.
